Personal Project Portfolio of Christos Katsampas

I’m someone who enjoys understanding how things work, why they work, and what makes them better. I’m particularly interested in the intersection of business, finance, and the broader forces shaping the economy — from the decisions companies make to the markets and policies that influence them. I believe in learning by doing. I enjoy taking an unfamiliar subject, breaking it down into its fundamentals, and developing the knowledge and tools to understand it independently. I value analytical thinking, intellectual curiosity, and the willingness to challenge my own assumptions. My goal is to keep expanding my perspective, take on increasingly complex challenges, and turn what I learn into work that is genuinely useful not just for myself but the world. This website is a record of that process: the projects I’ve completed, the questions I’m exploring, and the direction I’m working towards.
