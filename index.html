I’m someone who enjoys understanding how things work, why they work, and what makes them better. I’m particularly interested in the intersection of business, finance, and the broader forces shaping the economy — from the decisions companies make to the markets and policies that influence them.

I believe in learning by doing. I enjoy taking an unfamiliar subject, breaking it down into its fundamentals, and developing the knowledge and tools to understand it independently. I value analytical thinking, intellectual curiosity, and the willingness to challenge my own assumptions.

My goal is to keep expanding my perspective, take on increasingly complex challenges, and turn what I learn into work that is genuinely useful not just for myself but the world.

This website is a record of that process: the projects I’ve completed, the questions I’m exploring, and the direction I’m working towards.

About

I study Business Administration at the University of West Attica (UNIWA) and work at a business consulting boutique, where I prepare funding applications under Greek and EU programmes (ESPA / NSRF, the Development Law, the Recovery and Resilience Facility).

Outside client work I research energy markets and decarbonisation, from listed power producers to shipping regulation to large-scale orbital concepts.
