Decarbonisation investments in Greek shipping

Thesis for the Department of Business Administration, University of West Attica: a multi-criteria assessment of decarbonisation investments in Greek shipping under the EU ETS, FuelEU Maritime and the IMO Net-Zero Framework.

Research question

Greek shipowners control one of the world's largest merchant fleets, and three overlapping regimes now put a price on its emissions. The EU Emissions Trading System covers shipping emissions. FuelEU Maritime limits the greenhouse-gas intensity of energy used on board. The IMO Net-Zero Framework sets a global fuel standard with a pricing mechanism.

The thesis asks which decarbonisation investments make the most sense for Greek shipping under these regimes, when cost, compliance, technical and strategic criteria are weighed together.

Approach

  • Map each regime's obligations, timelines and cost exposure for a representative Greek fleet.
  • Define the candidate investments and the assessment criteria.
  • Rank the options with a multi-criteria decision analysis, then test how sensitive the ranking is to carbon prices and regulatory timing.
